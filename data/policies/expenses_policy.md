# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how employees are reimbursed for business expenses.

## Eligible Expenses

Eligible expenses include:

- Travel
- Accommodation
- Meals
- Transportation
- Business supplies

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Examples include:

- Alcohol
- Personal entertainment
- Fines
- Luxury items

## Submission Requirements

Employees must submit expenses:

- Within 30 days

Required documentation:

- Receipts
- Explanation
- Project code

## Approval

Expenses require manager approval.

Expenses above $500 require director approval.

## Travel Expenses

Employees must choose:

- Economy flights
- Standard hotels

## Meals

Meal limits:

- Breakfast: $15
- Lunch: $20
- Dinner: $40

## Payment

Reimbursement occurs within:

10 business days.

## Fraud

Expense fraud results in disciplinary action.
