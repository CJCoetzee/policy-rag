# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This policy defines acceptable workplace behavior.

## Professional Behavior

Employees must:

- Treat others respectfully
- Maintain professionalism
- Avoid harassment

## Harassment

Harassment includes:

- Verbal abuse
- Discrimination
- Bullying

Harassment is not tolerated.

## Equal Opportunity

Employment decisions are based on:

- Merit
- Qualifications
- Performance

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose conflicts.

Examples:

- Working for competitors
- Financial interests

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect company information.

## Social Media

Employees must not:

- Share confidential information
- Represent the company without approval

## Violations

Violations may result in:

- Warnings
- Suspension
- Termination
