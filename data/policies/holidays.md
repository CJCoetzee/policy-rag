# Company Holidays Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines official company holidays.

## Standard Holidays

Employees receive paid holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Good Friday
- Freedom Day
- Workers Day
- Youth Day
- National Women's Day
- Heritage Day
- Day of Reconciliation
- Christmas Day

## Holiday Pay

Employees are paid their normal salary.

## Weekend Holidays

If a holiday falls on a weekend:

The following Monday is observed.

## Working on Holidays

Holiday work requires:

- Manager approval

Employees receive:

- Double pay

or

- Time off in lieu.

## Additional Holidays

Management may announce additional holidays.
