# Business Travel Policy

## Purpose

Defines travel rules.

## Authorization

Travel requires:

Manager approval.

## Booking

Travel must be booked:

Through approved vendors.

## Flights

Employees must book:

Economy class.

Business class requires executive approval.

## Hotels

Employees must book:

Standard hotels.

## Transportation

Allowed:

- Taxi
- Rental cars
- Public transport

## Per Diem

Employees receive:

$60 per day.

## Safety

Employees must follow safety guidelines.

## International Travel

Requires:

Director approval.

## Insurance

Company provides travel insurance.
