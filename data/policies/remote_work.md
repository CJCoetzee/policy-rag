# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

This policy outlines the rules and expectations for remote work at Acme Corporation.

## Eligibility

Employees must:

- Complete probation
- Maintain satisfactory performance
- Have manager approval

## Remote Work Schedule

Employees may work remotely:

- Up to 3 days per week

Remaining days must be worked in the office.

## Fully Remote Employees

Fully remote arrangements require:

- Director approval
- HR approval

## Work Hours

Employees must be available:

09:00 – 16:00 local time.

Flexible hours outside core hours are permitted.

## Workspace Requirements

Employees must maintain:

- Reliable internet connection
- Quiet workspace
- Secure environment

## Equipment

Company provides:

- Laptop
- Charger
- Monitor (optional)

## Security Requirements

Employees must:

- Use VPN
- Lock screens
- Protect confidential data

## Performance Expectations

Remote employees must:

- Attend meetings
- Meet deadlines
- Respond to messages within 2 hours

## Revocation

Remote work privileges may be revoked if performance declines.
