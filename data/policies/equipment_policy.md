# Equipment Policy

## Purpose

Defines company equipment rules.

## Issued Equipment

Employees receive:

- Laptop
- Charger
- Accessories

## Ownership

All equipment remains company property.

## Care

Employees must:

- Avoid damage
- Prevent theft
- Store safely

## Loss

Lost equipment must be reported within 24 hours.

## Personal Use

Limited personal use allowed.

## Return

Equipment must be returned:

- On termination
- On role change

## Damage

Employees may be liable for negligence.
