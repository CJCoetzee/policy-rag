# Employee Benefits Policy

## Purpose

Defines employee benefits.

## Health Insurance

Full-time employees receive:

- Medical insurance

Company covers:

70% of premiums.

## Retirement

Employees receive:

- Pension contributions

Company contributes:

5% of salary.

## Wellness Benefits

Employees receive:

- Gym subsidy
- Wellness programs

## Education

Employees may receive:

- Training reimbursement

Limit:

$1000 per year.

## Eligibility

Benefits begin after:

90 days employment.
