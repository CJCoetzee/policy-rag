# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines information security standards at Acme Corporation.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must:

- Be at least 12 characters
- Include numbers and symbols
- Not be reused

Passwords must be changed every 180 days.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is required for:

- Email
- VPN
- Cloud systems

## Device Security

Employees must:

- Lock devices when unattended
- Install updates promptly
- Use antivirus software

## Acceptable Use

Company systems may not be used for:

- Illegal activity
- Unauthorized software
- Personal business

## Data Classification

Data categories:

### Public
Information safe for public release.

### Internal
Internal business information.

### Confidential
Sensitive business information.

### Restricted
Highly sensitive information.

## Data Storage

Confidential data must be stored only in approved systems.

Local storage is prohibited.

## Phishing

Employees must report suspicious emails immediately.

## Incidents

Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours.

## Violations

Security violations may result in termination.
