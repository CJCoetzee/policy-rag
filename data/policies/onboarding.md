# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines onboarding procedures.

## First Day

Employees must:

- Complete HR paperwork
- Receive equipment
- Attend orientation

## Training

Employees must complete:

- Security training
- Compliance training
- Role training

## Probation

Probation period:

3 months.

## Reviews

Managers conduct:

- 30-day review
- 90-day review

## Access

Employees receive:

- Email account
- System access
- ID badge

## Completion

Onboarding completes after:

- Training completed
- Manager approval
