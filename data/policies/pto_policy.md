# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define the paid time off benefits available to employees of Acme Corporation.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for paid time off benefits after completing 90 days of employment.

Part-time employees receive PTO on a prorated basis.

Contractors are not eligible for PTO benefits.

## Annual PTO Allowance

Employees receive PTO based on years of service:

| Years of Service | PTO Days |
|-----------------|----------|
| 0–2 years | 15 days |
| 3–5 years | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.

Unused PTO above this limit will expire on March 31.

## Requesting PTO

Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR system at least:

- 2 weeks in advance for planned leave
- 1 day in advance for short leave

Manager approval is required.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive:

- 10 sick days per year

Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Medical documentation may be required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave for:

- Family emergencies
- Bereavement
- Medical emergencies

Emergency leave is evaluated individually.

## Holidays

Public holidays are separate from PTO.

## Abuse of PTO

Misuse of PTO may result in disciplinary action.
